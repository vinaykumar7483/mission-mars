MISSION TRAJECTORY

01 // WHY MARS?

Mars is a near-term target for exploration and a compelling candidate for long-term human presence due to accessible polar ice, diverse geology, and the opportunity to advance human spaceflight systems.

02 // THE JOURNEY

Earth-to-Mars travel requires careful trajectory planning, radiation mitigation, life-support systems, and reliable propulsion.

03 // THE CHALLENGE

Low pressure, extreme cold, radiation, and resource scarcity make Mars a hard environment; engineering resilience is key.

04 // THE FUTURE

Long-term habitation requires closed-loop systems, energy resilience, and adaptable habitats—this project explores conceptual futures toward 2050.
